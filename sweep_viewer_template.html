The log has no units. V2 is read as volts and I2 as amps, shown here in µA. A sweep is one ramp of the DAC between its turning points (0 and 65,535); the log starts and ends mid-ramp, so the first and last sweeps are partial. Left and right arrow keys step through the sweeps.
