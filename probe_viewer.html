Reading the plasma potential

The plasma potential sits at the knee of the I–V curve, where the electron current stops rising exponentially. There dI/dV peaks and d²I/dV² crosses zero going from positive to negative. A crossing that moves or disappears when you change the smoothing is noise. If dI/dV climbs to a plateau without peaking, the sweep has no clear knee to read.

How the derivatives are computed

The source steps current in even increments, so V(I) is fitted with a quadratic over the smoothing window around each reading. The slopes are then inverted: dI/dV = 1 / (dV/dI) and d²I/dV² = −(d²V/dI²) / (dV/dI)³. A 3-point window passes exactly through neighbouring readings, so it applies no smoothing. Readings flagged at the source limit are left out.
